01 Subscription Products and Smart Search
Bonat B2B Loyalty / KSA Product Owner

Bonat is a Saudi B2B loyalty platform that lets merchants run loyalty programs without building their own. I own Subscription Products and Smart Search. The work covers scoping, prioritization, writing specifications, and user research. In the first six months I wrote over 20 user stories, ran 8 feedback interview sessions, and completed 3 feature researches.

02 Hail AI Adoption Initiative
Incubated by Misk Nonprofit / AI Vice Chairman

A nonprofit AI adoption initiative launching from Hail, incubated by Misk. We ran a field survey with 91 completed responses. It found high awareness and use of AI, and a gap in local mentorship, hands-on projects, and access to opportunities. 49 respondents asked to join. I work on positioning, vision and goals, budget, and building the team.

03 Khulasa, Arabic audio summaries
Closed July 2026 Started as a hackathon Consumer / Arabic Founder

An Arabic audio platform that turned long-form content into personalized summaries. It started at the Misk NextGenAI Hackathon in May 2024, where it placed third, then became a company. I led product, fundraising, and go-to-market with a team of three, and wrote the PRD, the business model, the competitor analysis, and the investor deck. I closed it in July 2026 because it needed more runway than I had.

04 Tura Hotels, web and booking rebuild
Five properties, Riyadh Hospitality Consultant

Five properties in Riyadh. The scope is a website rebuild, booking and payment integration, branch information, Google reviews, and chatbots on WhatsApp and Instagram. Their booking engine and payment gateway already run on SiteMinder, so the work is integration and user experience. I cover technical feasibility, scoping, phasing, and delivery.
